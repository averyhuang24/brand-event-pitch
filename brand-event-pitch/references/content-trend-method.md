# Content Trend 招商 Method

Use this reference when the user provides hot content, weekly topic notes, viral videos, livestream formats, variety shows, social platform cases, overseas content formats, AI玩法, festival behavior research, or asks for招商脑暴 based on current popular content.

The goal is not to copy a show or meme. The goal is to extract a repeatable content mechanism, then turn it into a brand-sellable campaign IP with user participation, scarce rights, resource amplification, and conversion/asset handoff.

## First Principle

Popular content becomes useful for招商 only when it answers four questions:

1. **What human tension makes people watch?** Examples: reversal, competition, companionship, practical help, identity mismatch, public judgment, healing, curiosity, social proof.
2. **What repeatable mechanism creates episodes?** Examples: contest, lab, ranking, table talk, limited-time solution, street operation, creator challenge, city route, audience submission.
3. **Where can brands enter naturally?** Brand must become a task, prop, scene, reward, solution, ingredient, gear, expert proof, route stop, product list, or challenge sponsor.
4. **How does attention become business value?** Connect to search, live, store/shelf, coupon, offline visit, list inclusion, content asset, PR report, or long-tail topic.

If a trend only provides a funny name but has no repeatable mechanism or brand role, treat it as inspiration, not an招商 idea.

## Trend Input Triage

When reading weekly content notes, classify each case into one of these buckets:

- **AI and new tools**: AI creators, AI short films, AI idols, AI hardware, product demos, hackathons, new creator roles.
- **Reversal and fish-out-of-water**: unexpected people placed in unexpected scenes, such as elite people doing grounded tasks or older/younger groups doing age-mismatched behaviors.
- **Contest and judgment**: cooking battles, product tournaments, ranking shows, creator competitions, public voting, sales/operation challenges.
- **Companionship and table/social gathering**: dinner tables, dorm rooms, friend groups, temporary partners, low-pressure social spaces.
- **Practical solution**: fast answers, home hacks, beauty/fashion fixes, money-saving, cleaning, cooking, health, skill verification.
- **Street/local operation**: pop-up stores, food trucks, city routes, neighborhood visits, merchant operation, real sales tests.
- **Wild micro-contest**: small offline competitions, odd challenges, local specialties, playful public participation.
- **Festival behavior**: how people now spend holidays, travel, gather, gift, eat, photograph, dress, decorate, or self-soothe.
- **Weather and timely triggers**: heat, cold, rain, back-to-school, sports events, commute, outdoor season, travel surge.

For each bucket, extract the mechanism, not just the title.

## High-Value Content Mechanisms

### 1. AI Creator / Product Arena

Best for: technology, electronics, appliances, beauty tech, smart home, creative tools, content platforms.

Mechanism:

- Recruit creators, products, teams, or tools.
- Give them a public challenge or commercial task.
- Show creation process, failure, iteration, judging, and final output.
- Let brands enter as challenge owners, product providers, tool partners, prize sponsors, or best-practice cases.

Sellable rights:

- category challenge sponsor
- creator/tool赛道 partner
- expert/jury naming
- product trial booth or livestream demo
- finalist content asset package
- award/report/PR inclusion

Avoid: saying "AI is hot" without defining what users or creators will produce.

### 2. Reversal Casting

Best for: content IP, seasonal campaigns, apparel, beauty, food, home, appliances, outdoor, local life.

Mechanism:

- Put a familiar identity into an unfamiliar task or scene.
- Make the contrast visible: age x behavior, profession x environment, status x hardship, expert x everyday problem.
- Use the tension to create comedy, empathy, or proof.

Sellable rights:

- task sponsor
- gear/product provider
- transformation package
- episode theme partner
- challenge reward
- behind-the-scenes content

Commercial logic: reversal creates watchability; brand products must help people complete the task or amplify the contrast.

### 3. Battle / Tournament / Judgment

Best for: food, apparel, beauty, home, electronics, mother/baby, pet, lifestyle goods.

Mechanism:

- Build factions, tracks, or identical starting conditions.
- Define a simple public standard: tasty, useful, stylish, affordable, durable, fast, relaxing, "worth buying".
- Use judges plus real users to produce argument, proof, and ranking.
- Connect winners to product list, livestream, coupon, or store module.

Sellable rights:

- track ownership
- product inclusion
- jury/expert co-creation
- public voting package
- winner list inclusion
- live-room battle day

Avoid: over-complex professional rules. The audience must understand the judgment standard instantly.

### 4. Gathering / Table / Dorm / Temporary Partner

Best for: food, beverage, snacks, mooncake/gifts, home, bedding, fragrance, small appliances, local life, travel.

Mechanism:

- Use a low-pressure social container: dinner table, dorm, rooftop, campsite, living room, night talk room, city walk team.
- Let hosts invite friends, strangers, creators, or users.
- Replace heavy drama with companionship, healing, ordinary stories, and small rituals.

Sellable rights:

- scene partner
- menu/snack/drink package
- host gift kit
- room/table setup
- episode topic sponsor
- offline gathering or livestream watch party

Commercial logic: the brand helps create the social atmosphere, not just appear on the table.

### 5. 60-Second Solution / Public Problem Solver

Best for: beauty, fashion, home cleaning, cooking, parenting, health, fitness, office efficiency, car, electronics.

Mechanism:

- Collect real user problems.
- Give a strict time limit or simple format for answers.
- Let experts, creators, or brands prove the solution on site.
- Invite users to submit, verify, remix, and challenge.

Sellable rights:

- problem category sponsor
- expert co-answer
- product proof slot
- user submission reward
- before/after content package
- solution card/list inclusion

Commercial logic: brand becomes the answer to a specific problem.

### 6. Street Operation / Real Business Challenge

Best for: food, retail, local life, home, outdoor, appliances, fashion, beauty pop-up, new product launch.

Mechanism:

- Put participants into real-world operating constraints: location, budget, time, weather, customer flow, inventory, sales target.
- Make business results visible: orders, queues, sell-through, repeat visits, ratings, live GMV, store visits.
- Let users decide through purchase, vote, visit, or review.

Sellable rights:

- store/booth partner
- product supply partner
- city route ownership
- sales challenge sponsor
- livestream operation day
- merchant/case report

Commercial logic: this format naturally turns content into business proof.

### 7. Wild Micro-Contest

Best for: offline events, city marketing, food, drinks, fruit, snacks, local specialties, festival nodes.

Mechanism:

- Take a small behavior and make it a public mini-competition.
- Keep the rule easy to understand and easy to film.
- Let creators, users, merchants, or brands host multiple local versions.

Sellable rights:

- contest naming
- prize/product provider
- city/merchant co-host
- creator challenge package
- offline booth integration
- highlight video and ranking

Commercial logic: small contests create low-cost participation and strong short-video moments.

### 8. Festival Re-Coding

Best for: holiday campaigns, gifts, food, beverage, travel, apparel, beauty, home, local life.

Mechanism:

- Do not assume the traditional holiday script still dominates.
- Split new behavior groups: family return, friend gathering, solo ritual, short trip, local night economy, culture experience, remote gifting, online companionship.
- Give each group a new ritual and a brand role.

Sellable rights:

- ritual track ownership
- gift/list inclusion
- city route or offline scene
- creator experience package
- live/store holiday day
- report/holiday behavior insight asset

Commercial logic: brand buys a role in the new holiday behavior, not just a seasonal ad slot.

## Conversion From Case To 招商 Idea

Use this five-step extraction:

1. **Name the watch reason**: Why did people care: reversal, contest, healing, useful answer, identity, local flavor, timely trigger?
2. **Define the engine**: What repeats every episode or every user participation?
3. **Map user action**: watch, submit, vote, try, buy, search, visit, remix, form a team, take a photo.
4. **Map brand role**: task owner, solution provider, scene builder, product proof, prize, judge, route owner, list partner.
5. **Map business handoff**: search, live, store/shelf, coupon, offline check-in, product list, PR, case video, report.

Only after these five steps should the agent name the campaign.

## Quality Gate

Before presenting an idea, check:

- It is not a direct copy of a named show or platform case.
- It has a repeatable content engine, not a one-off slogan.
- The brand role changes the content or user experience.
- There is at least one user action beyond passive viewing.
- There is a clear conversion or asset path.
- Scarcity is sellable: tracks, seats, list spots, city routes, challenge ownership, expert slots, scene ownership, or episode themes.
- The idea can produce memorable short-video/live moments.
