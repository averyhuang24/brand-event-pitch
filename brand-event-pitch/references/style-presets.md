# Public Style Presets

Use these built-in visual directions when no external template pack is available. They are not fixed templates; they are design recipes for generating original 16:9招商 slides.

These presets are image-led. For designed decks, pair each style with theme-relevant photography, generated campaign visuals, product/category imagery, UI mockups, route maps, or event scenes. Do not use these presets to create text-only decks.

## Selection Rule

Pick 3 distinct previews:

- one safe/commercial style
- one high-energy campaign style
- one wildcard style matched to the event theme

Never label slides with internal words like "preset", "template", "option", or the preset name. Use only real deck content on slides.

## Preset 1: Event Command Center

Best for: large campaign nodes, launch events, resource-heavy招商, growth plans.

Visual thesis: a high-clarity control room for a major commercial moment.

Design system:

- Background: deep charcoal or near-black with subtle grid, map lines, or dashboard ticks.
- Accent: electric red, signal green, bright cyan, or amber, but use one dominant accent.
- Typography: condensed bold headline, clean sans body.
- Layout: strong left title column + right data/roadmap panels.
- Image language: campaign command screens, event operations rooms, content dashboards, audience heat maps, live-room or search mockups.
- Signature elements: large numeric proof, timeline rails, resource blocks, small system labels.
- Avoid: cluttered dashboard cards, tiny charts, generic blue SaaS look.

Useful pages:

- opportunity proof
- resource matrix
- roadmap
- package comparison
- KPI or forecast page

## Preset 2: Cultural Pop IP

Best for: youth marketing, festival IP, city events, trend campaigns, social participation.

Visual thesis: a campaign world people want to join.

Design system:

- Background: saturated color field, poster texture, collage, halftone, or bold shape language.
- Accent: 2-3 contrasting colors, used with discipline.
- Typography: expressive display title + restrained body text.
- Layout: poster-like title pages, modular content scenes, big phrases, diagonal or layered composition.
- Image language: festival crowds, youth culture scenes, creator-style snapshots, city/street/event collages, playful product-in-scene visuals.
- Signature elements: stickers, badges, route maps, challenge cards, creator/avatar slots.
- Avoid: childish decoration, unreadable neon, using too many colors per page.

Useful pages:

- Big Idea
- user participation mechanism
- creator/UGC gameplay
- onsite event map
- brand co-creation examples

## Preset 3: Premium Partnership

Best for: high-budget sponsorship, luxury/auto/finance/B2B, strategic partnerships.

Visual thesis: confident, scarce, premium, and boardroom-ready.

Design system:

- Background: warm off-white, graphite, champagne, deep navy, or rich black.
- Accent: gold, copper, silver, or a single brand-compatible color.
- Typography: elegant serif or high-contrast display for titles; refined sans for body.
- Layout: generous spacing, editorial grids, large imagery, restrained data.
- Image language: cinematic product/lifestyle photography, premium venue scenes, partner portraits, detail crops, editorial case imagery.
- Signature elements: thin rules, section numerals, cinematic image crops, quiet rights tables.
- Avoid: overdecorated luxury clichés, low-contrast text, vague mood pages without business proof.

Useful pages:

- executive opener
- market insight
- premium rights package
- brand role page
- case/proof page

## Preset 4: Trust Lab

Best for: product proof, source/origin stories, quality inspection, tech validation, credibility campaigns.

Visual thesis: evidence becomes desire.

Design system:

- Background: clean white, lab gray, translucent panels, document layers.
- Accent: verification green, blue, black, or highlighter yellow.
- Typography: clear geometric sans, tabular numbers.
- Layout: evidence cards, before/after comparison, inspection flow, proof wall.
- Image language: lab/process imagery, source/origin photos, product closeups, factory/craft/ingredient scenes, proof screenshots.
- Signature elements: stamps, checkmarks, specimen labels, torn-paper reveals, test result strips.
- Avoid: sterile corporate blandness; every proof page still needs a human reason to care.

Useful pages:

- consumer trust tension
- proof mechanism
- expert/creator validation
- brand claim-to-evidence map
- media/authority endorsement

## Preset 5: Retail Heatwave

Best for: sales conversion campaigns, live commerce, shopping festivals, coupon/package招商.

Visual thesis: a commercial heat map where attention becomes action.

Design system:

- Background: warm gradient or bold red/orange/pink field balanced with black/white.
- Accent: price-tag yellow, signal red, hot pink, or lime.
- Typography: bold condensed headlines, oversized numbers, punchy labels.
- Layout: funnel flow, conversion ladder, hero offer blocks, product/category tiles.
- Image language: shopping scenes, product grids, live-room frames, deal modules, category tiles, crowd/action buying moments.
- Signature elements: heat meters, countdowns, deal tags, burst shapes, live-room frames.
- Avoid: cheap sale poster aesthetics; keep enough polish for brand decision-makers.

Useful pages:

- conversion opportunity
- live/search/store mechanism
- category package
- timeline to sale
- business outcome page

## Preset 6: City Route / Experience Map

Best for: offline events, city activations, tourism, lifestyle route, roadshow, campus, mall/street campaigns.

Visual thesis: the brand joins a route, not a booth.

Design system:

- Background: map texture, route lines, location cards, large atmospheric image.
- Accent: wayfinding colors: orange, blue, green, or black/yellow.
- Typography: strong signage-like title, legible body.
- Layout: route map, station cards, day plan, touchpoint matrix.
- Image language: city/place photos, route scenes, venue/event photos, map screenshots, station cards, onsite activation renders.
- Signature elements: pins, tickets, stamps, route arrows, location labels, itinerary strips.
- Avoid: literal tourist brochure look unless the event brief calls for it.

Useful pages:

- experience overview
- offline map
- brand touchpoints
- creator route
- onsite-to-online amplification

## Fixed Slide Rules

- Design at 1920x1080.
- No scrolling inside slides.
- Titles should make a claim, not name a section.
- Resource tables must stay readable at presentation size.
- Use meaningful images on most non-table slides.
- If a slide has more than 6 dense bullets, split it.
- Use real campaign phrases and commercial claims; never show internal design notes.
